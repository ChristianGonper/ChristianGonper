## Selected projects

### [Neural control of a quadcopter](https://github.com/ChristianGonper/modelado-control-dron-v2)

My undergraduate thesis investigates whether neural networks can replace specialised position controllers for trajectory tracking. I designed a six-degree-of-freedom simulator and compared classical and neural control under reproducible conditions. The study was conducted in simulation, and the results depend on the trajectory evaluated.

[Project and thesis](https://christiangonper.github.io/modelado-control-dron-v2/) · [Repository](https://github.com/ChristianGonper/modelado-control-dron-v2)

### [SkyBrief](https://github.com/ChristianGonper/briefing-meteorologico-aeronautico)

An aeronautical weather application for exploring flight conditions across Europe. It brings together METAR and TAF reports, weather maps and route profiles, with indicators for hazards such as icing and wind shear.

[Explore SkyBrief](https://github.com/ChristianGonper/briefing-meteorologico-aeronautico)
